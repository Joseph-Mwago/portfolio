Featured Project

Automated Cake Frosting Machine

Final Year Project · 2026 · Team of 2 · 7 months

Designed and built a working multi-axis CNC frosting machine that successfully frosts real cakes up to 8" diameter — improving on a prior system that couldn't handle high-viscosity buttercream.

  • Designed a Cartesian flying-gantry + Theta rotary platform in CAD, fabricated from welded mild steel with belt-driven X/Y and dual lead-screw Z axes
  • Engineered a positive-displacement piston extruder (106 mm PPR reservoir, dual synchronized lead screws) to overcome buttercream's yield stress and shear-thinning behaviour, modelled using the Herschel-Bulkley flow equation
  • Developed custom ESP32-S3 firmware in PlatformIO (C++) with modular motion, extrusion, Theta, sensor, and sequencing modules
  • Designed and fabricated a custom PCB for power distribution (24 V / 5 V / 3.3 V domains), motor drivers, and sensor integration
  • Built and integrated 7 subsystems into a working prototype — frame, X/Y/Z motion, rotary platform, extrusion, electronics, software
  • Used OpenSCAD for parametric modelling and prototyping, and LLMs for firmware development and debugging
  • Fabricated custom components using 3D printing, lathe, welding, and woodwork
